# Contributing to the SPEX project - 2026

The SPEX project welcomes new contributions and contributors under
the terms of the [Contributor Covenant CoC](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
and the following terms.

# Principles, Goals and Constituencies

The primary goal of this project is to create systems that the human
operators and creators of human-scale computing can more easily
read, understand, and share with their communities.

That is to say:

- Simplicity is more important than completeness.
- Legibility is more important than performance.
- Accessibility is more important than security.
- Integrity is more important than automation.

The most important constituencies of this project are - first - the
human operators of single computers and - second - the communities
they participate in or support. While other constituencies exist
in computing - governments, corporations, hyperscalers, among others
- their operational or licensing needs are not the priority of this
project.

# The Contribution Process

While this project welcomes contributions and new contributors, I
insist that any contributions larger than a few lines - in particular
any new features or significant rearchitecting or reorganization
of the codebase - *must* begin with a discussion in the issue tracker
that establishes goals, scope, constraints and testing required for
that change to be accepted.

The software _per se_ is not the goal. Code is the implementation
language of a shared understanding, and that shared understanding
of a shared problem, between people with wants and needs living in
the world, is the goal. That communities can gather around that
shared understanding to benefit from it, improve their own situation
or better the state of the world together, is the goal.

# Constraints

Because creating code is so much easier than understanding, debugging
and maintaining that code, particularly over the long term, large
contributions offered before we've come to some reasonable consensus
about structure, success conditions and acceptance criteria are not
welcome. Because the priority of this project is a shared human
understanding, 'agentic', LLM or sochastially-generated contributions
will be unilaterally r rejected.
